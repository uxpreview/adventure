# PLAY SHEET — Session 16, THE FIRST HOUR

*Ten minutes. The play gate is yours (`QUALITY-BAR.md` §2): no tool in
this repository can say whether any of this is fun, and this session
did not run the gate. It hands you the script.*

**The build:** https://adventure-git-claude-session-16-8idifp-ryankm.vercel.app
(the branch's preview). Once merged it is
[adventure.ryankm.com](https://adventure.ryankm.com). **Play on a fresh
page** — SET OUT, not KEEP WALKING. The whole point of this session is
the first minute, and a save skips it. (If you want the first minute
twice, clear the site's storage; nothing else resets it.)

---

## 1. Wake. (0:00)

You are not at the signpost. You are in long grass, and there is a bull
to your right, looking at you. **Do nothing for a second.** Then run:
`Shift` and `A` (or drag past the ring, to the left). West. There is a
gate in the hedge ahead of you with somebody at it.

*Ask:* did you know to run? Did the hint come at the right moment, or
did the bull? Did the bull frighten you, and for how long? Did it ever
feel like it would catch you? (It will not. It stops two strides short,
every time, and nothing says so.)

## 2. The gate. (0:10)

Go through the gate. **She shuts it behind you.** The bull stops at the
hedge, to your right, and snorts, and after a while goes back to its
grass.

*Ask:* did you see her shut it, or only hear it? Did you see the bull
stop? Is the hedge a wall or a rule? Turn round and walk back to the
gate — it is shut. There is a stile further along the fence if you
want the bull again. Did you want the bull again?

## 3. Four things. (0:30)

Walk north-west to the crossroads — forty units, the road carries you.
Stand on the ring at the signpost and look. The castle, straight up the
road. A plume of smoke to the right of the town. A run of pale towers
further right. And a horizon to the left, above the oaks, with light on
it. The signpost points at three of them.

**On a phone, hold two fingers on the screen and lean left, then
right.** The frame is narrow: you get the castle at rest, the sea and
the smoke on a lean, and the towers not at all. That is measured
(`tools/check-lures.mjs`) and it is honest; tell me if it is enough.

*Ask:* did you know where to go? Did the four things read as things,
or as pencil in the sky? Which road did you pick, and why that one?

## 4. The goat. (1:00)

Somewhere between the gate and the crossroads a goat fell in behind you.
Walk any road out of the Common — north to Brim is shortest. **It
follows you to the border and stops dead.** Keep walking. Look back
(you cannot; walk south and it is still there, at the edge).

*Ask:* did you notice it following? Did you notice it stop? Did the
stop feel like a rule or like a goat? Did you go back for it?

## 5. Nell. (3:00)

Walk back to the gate in the hedge. The prompt says **LEAN ON THE GATE
WITH HER**. Read it. Then go round the fence to the hay cart (over the
stile, or along the east road and round the fence's east end) and
**push the cart** a few shoves, any direction. Walk back to the gate.

She is not leaning any more. She is standing, and she is looking at
where you put the cart.

*Ask:* did you see the change in her? (Three signals: her posture, her
feet, and she has stepped off the post.) Is it enough? Did you feel
watched, or judged, or nothing?

## 6. The card. (6:00)

**This one needs the far corner of the world**, so it is for a second
sitting: walk the whole line to THE 8:15 STOP in the Cubicle Mile, read
the timetable, and come back to Nell's gate. The prompt says **TELL HER
THE FOURTH NAME**, and it is a card with two doors: tell her, or keep
it and push the cart yourself.

- Tell her: the cart is loaded, roped, and turned north, at the gate,
  and it does not move. She stands and looks at it.
- Keep it: nothing happens to the cart but what you do to it. Push it
  to any border; it stops there; she does not follow it, and she has
  stopped watching the road.

*Ask:* did you know what each door cost before you chose? Which did
you choose? Nothing says which was right. Should it?

## 7. Districts and the fair. (8:00)

Walk the Common. Crossing into the well, the crossroads, the river bend
or the fair ground deals a smaller card with the land's name over the
district's. The fair ground is south-west of the crossroads: a ring in
the grass, a maypole, a board. Read the board. Open the map (`M`): the
Common has four dashed districts drawn inside it now, named.

*Ask:* do the district cards help you know where you are, or are they
noise? Is four right for a land this size?

## What I could not judge, and am not claiming

- Whether any of it is **fun**. That is the gate, and it is yours.
- Whether the bull frightens, or for how long. On the harness the gate
  is shut at 6.6 seconds; on your thumb it may be fifteen.
- Whether the goat's stop READS as a rule (`critique-story-3`).
- Whether the four lures read as four things at the edge of a frame,
  especially on a phone with a lean.
- **The ear gate, still.** Four new sounds in `out/sound/`: the snort,
  the hooves, the gate, the goat. Unheard, like the fifty-eight before.
- **The feel gate, still**, plus one new thing: the run taught under
  pressure, with the camera not helping by turning.

Everything above is measured where it can be (`tools/check-verbs.mjs`,
`tools/check-lures.mjs`) and photographed both ways
(`tools/shoot-session16.mjs`). None of that is a verdict.
