# THE BLEACH FLATS — land spec

*Session 11. The flattest ground in the world, and the only land whose
whole thesis is that the answer is somewhere else. `THE-WAITS.md` §5 is
binding: **the cistern has always been full, and he is not faking a
rainfall.***

Rect: x 230 → 380, z −100 → 130. Wash `WASH.desert`. Voice: air, third
register — *wind with nothing left to move but the top of the page:
high, thin, and unanswered* (`Audio.ts`, `LAND_VOICE`).

---

## 1. THE SHOT

**Stand on the track at (303, 76) and look north.** You are twenty units
south of the only water in the land and you are on the only worn ground
in it, and both of those facts are one person's.

| layer | what |
|---|---|
| **foreground** | the track under the lens — a strip of scuffed ground one person wide, with no edge on it, running away north. It is the only worn thing in the Bleach Flats |
| **near** | **one water can**, standing at the side of it. He leaves one at each end, because carrying an empty can forty units is work for nothing, and nothing in this game will ever say so |
| **subject** | **THE OASIS.** The pool reads as a hard blue line across the flat, with reeds on the near bank — the south bank, which is the only bank they are on |
| **middle** | the palms, all eight of them, standing BEHIND the water. Every one of them is on the north shore |
| **far** | the flattest horizon in the game, and the world's curled rim away to the right |
| **accent** | the blue. It is the only blue in a hundred and fifty units in any direction, and `THE-STRANGERS` U21 is that it is the same blue as the sea and it is nowhere near it |
| **moving** | the fronds; one tumbleweed crossing on the one wind; the dust devil, somewhere, going away |

**AND THE SAME PLACE FROM THE OTHER SIDE IS NOT THERE.** The palms are
massed on the NORTH shore, so a player who walks off the east road and
comes at it from the north finds a stand of trees with nothing under
them and no water anywhere, because the water is on the far side of them
and the ground is flat (`THE-STRANGERS` C22). **The land teaches you
that you came at it from the wrong side**, which is the belief of the
place said in a placement rule and not in a note.

**THE GROUND IS THE SIXTH PLACE.** `elevation.ts` gained **THE PAN** —
one dish, ninety units across and a unit and three quarters deep, and it
is the only shape in the land. The oasis sits at the bottom of it and
**Amos's cistern sits on its rim**, which means the forty units he
carries by hand every night are forty units UPHILL, both ways, for as
long as he has been doing it. Nobody will ever measure that and it is
true anyway. (A unit and three quarters over forty-five is a thirtieth
of the hatching threshold, so the dish may be doubly curved here for the
same reason the tarn's bowl may be in the Penwood — there is no fall
line for the shader to draw down. It is bounded on all four sides, hard,
at x = 332: twelve units short of where the curl starts lifting and
thirty-eight short of the protected `curl-rim` framing's own ground.)

## 3. COMPOSITION PLAN

**The rule, and it is the canyon's turned inside out:**

> **NOTHING IN THIS LAND IS ENCLOSED AND NOTHING IN IT IS TALL. THERE
> ARE FOUR THINGS TO WALK TO AND EIGHTY UNITS BETWEEN THEM.**

The emptiness is the content. What makes it composed emptiness rather
than an empty rect is that **everything worth walking to is on one
axis** — the track, x ≈ 303 — and everything off that axis is
deliberately, visibly not worth the walk.

- **the ground** is a density gradient, not a coverage: thickest along
  the north where the road comes in and the page still remembers the
  Downs, and thinning to almost nothing over the pan, which is THE PALE
  and is supposed to be bare. The dune script runs ONE WAY: there is one
  wind out here and a floor combed at random angles is a floor nothing
  combed.
- **the scrub** grows in eight clusters and nowhere else, because a
  desert plant grows where the last one dropped.
- **the palms** are one mass on one shore, which is the whole of C22.
- **two composed voids:** the south third going sour toward the city
  (`sourGround` in `elevation.ts` already dishes it), and the west
  quarter, where the only thing to see is the river going away.

**Cut from the draft:** sixty dune decals, forty-two cacti and eight
tumbleweeds on a Poisson scatter, four palms in a ring round the pond,
and two cow skulls with horns.

## 4. INK TECHNIQUE

> **Everything in THE BLEACH FLATS is drawn in strokes ACROSS the page,
> and every one of them gives up as it rises.**

SPLITROCK is drawn entirely in verticals because a tear runs the way its
fibres run. The Flats are its opposite in every other respect and they
are its opposite here: dune script, crack, dash and rule — long, level,
broken runs laid one under another, with not a vertical mark on the
ground anywhere in the land.

The second half is the heat. **THE PALING IS THE LAND.** `pale()` takes
the ink out of the top of every drawing that STANDS, so a post is black
at its foot and nearly gone at its top and a palm's crown is the
faintest thing in the frame. It costs an alpha ramp per drawing and it
is why the Flats do not look like the Downs painted a different colour.
*(Round 1 shipped it at more than twice this strength and the palms
disappeared entirely — see §10.)*

## 5. MOTION & LIFE

- **the tumbleweeds** — nine, crossing the land west to east on the one
  wind and wrapping round to the far side when they get there. **One of
  them is caught on the fence posts and only rocks**, and has been for a
  while.
- **the fronds** — per-instance wind on the palms and the reeds, which
  is the only green movement in eighty units.
- **AMOS's night** — from half past eight in the evening to half past
  four he is on the track and he is on it the whole time: down empty,
  back with two full cans, and again, one round trip every eighty
  game-minutes. `THE-STRANGERS` C21 is a player meeting him going the
  other way, and the only way to make that happen is for it to be true.
- **PLAYER-RESPONSIVE: THE DUST DEVIL.** One thin column of lifted grit
  that crosses the Flats on its own bearing and **leans away from
  anybody who walks at it** — never fast, never a flee, just always
  further off than it was. It is the one thing out here that is going
  somewhere and you cannot reach it, which is `THE-WAITS` §5's belief
  drawn instead of written: *the answer is elsewhere.*

## 6. SOUND

Voice and bed unchanged: air, high, and the thinnest bed in the game.
**Nothing out here comes back.** The canyon repeats you; this land has
nothing to repeat you off, and every voice in it arrives from one side
and leaves by the other.

| event | trigger | synthesis |
|---|---|---|
| `palm-rattle` | within 26 units of the oasis, gaps shortening as you close | five to ten dry knocks over half a second with a hiss under them. **The only sound in the Bleach Flats made by something alive**, and you hear it before you can see the water — which is what makes the oasis arrive rather than appear |
| `can-knock` | on the track, at night | one hollow knock, its answer half a second later on the other side of him, and the slop of something nearly full |
| `grit-run` | anywhere else | one noise band sweeping down and away, and not returning |

Step zone `sand`, unchanged.

**THE CATCH's label sits east of the apron at height 3.2**, off its axis:
the apron is thirteen units wide and eight and a half tall and the
cistern stands in front of the middle of it, so a name written over the
tank lands on the deck from anywhere on the track. Angle, not height.

**Three things the notes never say**, and all three are in the geometry:
that he carries the water; that the gutter runs downhill FROM the
cistern and could therefore never have filled it (`THE-STRANGERS` U23);
and that the columns on the board are empty because there has never been
anything to put in them (U22). There is **no count, no list and no
number** anywhere in this land, the rain table included.

## 10. WHAT THE GATE FOUND, IN ORDER

1. **THE PALING ATE THE DRAWINGS.** Round 1's `pale()` erased 72% of the
   ink over the bottom two thirds of every canvas. The palms lost their
   crowns entirely and kept their trunks, which were drawn at full
   ballpoint in INK — so a desert palm came out as a black pole with a
   ghost on top of it. The technique was right and the numbers were
   three times too strong; the trunk is brown now and the crown
   survives.
2. **A LOZENGE IS NOT A CISTERN.** Same root cause as the canyon's
   (`stroke` rounds corners), plus one of its own: round 2 drew the tank
   as a box with a flat lid laid on it, which is a COTTAGE. What makes a
   cistern a cistern is that its top is a ring seen from slightly above.
3. **THE APRON WAS IN THE LENS OF THE LAND'S OWN SHOT.** It stood on the
   track's line eight units north of the cistern — which is exactly where
   the camera sits when a walker stands on the track and looks at the
   oasis, so THE SHOT came back as a close-up of a boarded deck. **The
   camera is fourteen units behind the walker and everything behind the
   walker is in front of the camera.**
4. **A MAN CROUCHING READS AS AN ANIMAL AT FORTY UNITS** if his head
   goes below his hips. He stands at the tank for two thirds of the day
   now, which is also the truer thing: there is not much to do to a
   machine that works.
5. **C22 CANNOT BE PHOTOGRAPHED STANDING STILL.** The whole of *the
   oasis, from the wrong direction, and it is not there* is that you are
   walking AWAY from the water while the camera looks the way it always
   looks. It is a DRIVEN framing or it is nothing.
