# THE BLEACH FLATS — land spec

*Session 11, and the opposite of the land it ships beside: SPLITROCK is
the most vertical thing on the sheet and this is the flattest ground in
the world. `THE-WAITS.md` §5 is binding: **the cistern is full, it has
always been full, and Amos is not faking a rainfall — he is keeping the
thing that catches rain in working order.***

Rect: x 230 → 380, z −100 → 130. Wash `WASH.desert`. Step `sand`.
Voice: air, third register — the thinnest thing in the score, in the
land with the least in it (`Audio.ts`, `LAND_VOICE`).

**The land's whole thesis is that the answer is somewhere else**, and
every decision below serves that: the ground is shaped to hold water and
holds none, the only water in it is somewhere else, and the one person
here has built a machine to receive something that is not coming and
keeps it perfect.

---

## 1. THE SHOT

**Stand at THE CATCH (301, 95) and look north.** This is the whole fable
in one frame, and not one word of it is written down.

| layer | what |
|---|---|
| **foreground** | the cistern's lid and the guttering coming down off its frame, at the frame's left, cropped, at full pressure. Square, straight, graded, painted, and there is not a leaf in the trap |
| **near** | the ground falling away from you — one and seven tenths of a unit of authored swell, which is why you can see anything at all |
| **subject** | **THE TRACK.** A bare worn line with two edges, running dead up the middle of the frame, going away |
| **middle** | nothing. Forty units of pale ground with nothing on it, which is the point and is composed rather than left over |
| **far** | **THE OASIS**: a smudge of green at the end of the track. The only colour anywhere in the land |
| **horizon** | flat, and then the world's curled east rim a long way off on the right hand, in haze |
| **accent** | the green, and it is forty units away |
| **moving** | the palms, and nothing near you. The lid is on |

**A man, a track, and where the water actually comes from, in one
composition.** Nobody says it. The note at the catch states three
things — the guttering is straight, it has not rained here in anyone's
memory, the cistern is full — and draws no conclusion at all.

**After you have walked the crease, both faces,** the same frame has one
difference. **The lid is off, leaning against the cistern,** and you can
see the water in it. He has decided to find out. The game does not say
whether that is despair or nerve.

**AND THERE IS A SECOND SHOT AND IT IS ONLY AVAILABLE AT NIGHT.** Stand
anywhere on the track after the light goes and Amos is on it, with a
yoke, going one way or the other. He does it every night. Nothing in the
game tells you to come back after dark, and if you never do you will
simply think the cistern is full.

## 3. COMPOSITION PLAN

**Cut from the draft:** sixty dune decals on a Poisson scatter;
forty-two saguaros on another; two loose skulls; and the eight
tumbleweeds that marched east at `2.2 + i × 0.24` units a second and
teleported back to x = 236 — which is an array that moves, and is worse
than an array that does not.

**The rule that replaces it:** *nothing in the Flats is scattered.
Everything loose has been SORTED, and it has been sorted the same way,
by the same wind, for a very long time.*

- **THE STRAND LINES.** Three of them, concentric on the pan, each one a
  place a wash stopped. Every loose thing in the land lives on one:
  the grit, the dead scrub, the bleached wood. Between them the ground
  is bare, and it is bare because things have been taken off it.
- **THE THREE SAGUAROS.** In a row, thirty units apart, on the pan's
  outer strand line, and there is nothing anywhere else in the land
  taller than a knee. They are standing on the ghost of a shoreline.
  Three, in a line, is not an array — it is a fact about where water
  was.
- **THE LEE.** Anything that stands has a tail of grit behind it on the
  same bearing, always. One vector, the whole land.
- **Density gradient:** heaviest at the pan's rim and along the track;
  nothing at all in the pan's middle, and nothing at all in the east
  third. The oasis is a single dense knot, twelve units across, and it
  is the only knot in a hundred and fifty units.
- **Occlusion:** the Flats have almost no near layer, which is a problem
  a flat land always has. The answer is the CATCH: it is the only
  foreground element in the land and every framing that needs weight
  puts it in the corner.

## 4. INK TECHNIQUE

**THE FLATS ARE DRAWN IN HORIZONTALS AND THE CANYON IS DRAWN IN
VERTICALS.** One prop box, `textures-dry.ts`, so the difference is
authored rather than accidental.

**And the land's signature is not a shape, it is a PRESSURE: heat is
line weight.** The pen presses hardest where the water is — full weight
on the oasis's reeds and on Amos's guttering — and lightens by a third
for every twenty units out, until in the middle of the pan the marks are
barely there and the paper is doing most of the work. Nothing is
faded with alpha; the STROKE is thinner and its passes drop from two to
one. That is what a bleached page looks like and it is not a filter.

## 5. MOTION & LIFE

- **The palms.** The only moving green in the land, at the far end of
  THE SHOT. `wind` on a field of three.
- **The shimmer.** The dead scrub carries `wind` with a very small
  amplitude and a high frequency, which at a hundred units reads as heat
  rather than as movement. Vertex stage, free.
- **The drift.** Four tumbleweeds, each with its own path, its own
  pauses (a weed hangs up on scrub and then goes on) and the same
  bearing as everything else in the land. They are not a convoy.
- **AMOS, AND HE IS THE MOTION THAT MATTERS.** By day he is at the
  catch, working the length of the guttering. **After the light goes he
  is on the track with a yoke, going one way or the other, and he does
  it every night.** That is `THE-WAITS` §5's turn, shown, in the world,
  on the clock Session 6 built — and it is the single strongest argument
  in this project for having a day cycle at all.
- **THE PLAYER-RESPONSIVE ONE:** come near a hung-up tumbleweed and it
  goes. It is small and it is the only thing in the Flats that reacts to
  a person, which is itself the land saying something.

## 6. SOUND

Mood `desert` (phrygian shadow, sparse, gap 10s) unchanged. Bed:
`bandpass 1500` with a thirteen-second swell — wind with nothing in it.

| event | trigger | synthesis |
|---|---|---|
| `dry-wind` | the land's default idle | a long filtered `surge`, no pitch centre, coming from nowhere and going nowhere |
| `palm-clatter` | within about twenty-two units of the oasis | six dry `knock`s, uneven, high, falling away — the only thing in the land that sounds alive |
| `catch-tick` | within about sixteen units of the cistern, and **only in the two hours after the light goes** | four thin metallic `tone`s at long uneven gaps. Tin cooling. It is a sound a thing in good order makes and nothing else in this land makes any sound at all |

Step zone: `sand`, which is already the region's.

**THE CATCH's note is the load-bearing one and it is three sentences of
fact with no conclusion in it.** `THE-WAITS` §5 lists what this land
never says: that it will not rain, that he carries the water, that
anybody has noticed. None of the five notes says any of the three, and
the note at the catch is written specifically so that a player who has
not been here at night reads it as a man keeping his gear nice.

**MEASURED** (`tools/shoot-fps.mjs`, portrait): the Flats' worst framing
is **98 draws / 1.1 ms** — against THE COMMON's 261, Brim Square's 259
and the Downs' 238. **They are the cheapest land in the game to draw by
a factor of two and a half, and they should stay that way**: this is
where a mid-range phone gets its headroom back after the canyon, and it
is not an accident — it is what a composed void costs.

## 9. NEW ENGINE NEEDS

None.

**AND TWO GROUND TERMS, WHICH ARE THE WHOLE OF THIS LAND'S ELEVATION AND
THERE WILL NEVER BE A THIRD:** THE PAN (a two-and-a-half-unit dish at
(272, 58), the lowest ground in the east half of the world, with a
strand line round it) and THE CATCH'S RISE (one and seven tenths at
(300, 96), because a rain-catch goes on the highest ground a man can
find). Both are gaussian in x AND z, so both are bounded on all four
sides by construction and the harrow's mistake cannot be made with a
bump. A third, THE OASIS' HOLLOW, was added at round 2 of the gate.

**One thing this land needed that did not exist, and it is not an engine
need — it is content:** `fact:the-fold`. `THE-WAITS` §0 has said since
Session 7 that the Flats' wait turns on it, and no such id existed and
nothing taught it. It is authored in `knowledge.ts` as a two-post route
on the crease's two shoulders, a hundred and eighty units apart, in two
lands this session did not build. **A wait whose key is in somebody
else's land is the content system working**, and this is the first one
in the game where the earning and the spending were authored in the same
session and in different halves of the world.
