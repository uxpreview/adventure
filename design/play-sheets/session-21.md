# PLAY SHEET — Session 21, THE SECOND DOOR

*Ten minutes, the five cards, both doors on each, and then the train.
The play gate is yours (`QUALITY-BAR.md` §2): no tool in this repository
can say whether any of this is fun, and this session did not run the
gate. It hands you the script.*

**The build:** the branch's Vercel preview,
[adventure-git-claude-next-session-jaghgc-ryankm.vercel.app](https://adventure-git-claude-next-session-jaghgc-ryankm.vercel.app).
Once merged it is [adventure.ryankm.com](https://adventure.ryankm.com).

**Every wait in the world has two doors on a card now.** The five that
did not — Marget's clock, Holt's boat, Amos's cistern, Brack's tarn,
Joan's table — do from this session, and the 8:15 reads every door
back. **One thing to decide is yours and is in §7 below.**

**The address bar still works:** `?hour=19.4` for the lamps coming on,

stop when it comes.

## 1. Marget's clock. (`?hour=19.3`, 0:00)

Brim, the belfry yard, the north-west corner of the square. Stand by
the tower while the lamps come on (they come on one at a time, from
the south-west; the man lighting them is four lamps behind). **SETTLE
THE HOUR.** Three doors. Read all three before you pick.

*Ask:* did you know which hand was the lamps'? Did that make the
choice easier or worse? Pick one. Then walk the square. (Eight: the
market is open and her stall is not, and never will be. Eleven: she
opens, and come back at `?hour=17` — the lamps are lit in broad
daylight, every day, from now on. The bell: the wait as it was.
Nothing says which was right.)

## 2. Holt's sea. (`?hour=12`, 2:00)

Splitrock, the head of the channel. You need to have rowed the river
(or trust the sheet). **TELL HIM WHAT YOU ROWED.** Two doors.

*Ask:* did you want to tell him about the sea? Did it feel like a
kindness or a cruelty? (Told, he stops oiling. The stain is gone off
the hull, the chalk marks on the wall are going, and he is up at the
house all day. He does not come down when you walk up the channel any
more.)

## 3. Amos's cistern. (`?hour=12`, 3:30)

The Flats, the catch at the south end of the track. You need the fold
(or trust the sheet). **TELL HIM ABOUT THE FOLD.** Take the second
door. Then: **there is a can at the foot of the tank. PICK IT UP.**
Walk it down the track to the water — forty units — FILL THE CAN, and
walk it back up. Every unit is uphill. EMPTY IT IN.

*Ask:* did the walk back feel like forty units? Did it feel like his?
Was that the point? (He stops carrying. Come back at `?hour=23`: the
track is empty, and by day it is growing over. The lid stays on.)

## 4. Brack's oar. (`?hour=12`, 6:00)

The Penwood, off the round toward the water. Before you are anywhere
near the tarn: **GO DOWN TO THE WATER.** Two doors. Take the oar.
Walk to the boat, PICK UP THE OAR, and carry it back to the woodcutter
at the lean. **STAND IT WITH THE OTHERS.**

*Ask:* did you count them afterwards? Did you look at Brack? (He has
not turned. Read THE TARN's note. Twelve oars, and one of them is
right, and nobody says which.)

## 5. Joan's table. (`?hour=11.8`, 7:30)

The Downs, the headland by the mill. **SIT DOWN, OR CLEAR IT.** Two
doors.

*Ask:* did you hesitate? Clear it, then sit down anyway. (One plate.
The seat is still a seat. Nothing happens. That is the choice with no
loser but you.)

## 6. The train reads the doors. (`?hour=8.2`, 8:30)

If you have walked the line and decided seven waits — either door
counts now — stand at the Flats' stop (the east–west leg of the road
through Maple Court, `x 40`) and wait for a quarter past eight.

*Ask:* what was on the platform? (Two cans, if you filled the tank.
Seven pots at Longshore's stop, if you hauled them. Nobody at
Splitrock's, if you told him. Marget at Brim's, if her hand won.)
Come back the next morning: **the cans are still on the platform.**

## 7. THE ONE THING THAT IS YOURS TO DECIDE

**Does the departure last?** Today, when the doors shut, everybody who
got on is back where they stand — Marget at her stall the next
morning, Wick on the avenue. This session built the smaller
permanence: what a door LEFT on a platform stays there for good. The
larger — the people who got on are gone from their lands, and their
routines with them — is one clause per land and re-opens seven lands
that hold verdicts, and it is in `PROMPT.md` for Session 22 with your
name on the decision. **Say yes, no, or which lands.**

## What moved, and what I could not judge

Five lands re-opened — BRIM, SPLITROCK, THE FLATS, THE PENWOOD, THE
DOWNS — and the 8:15. `diff-sheets` reports the protected set in
`SESSIONS.md`; Brim's four protected framings are on a page that has
taken no door.

- Whether any door is a **choice** rather than a completion. That is
  the gate, and it is yours.
- **Whether the carry is fun** (Amos's forty units, Brack's forty
  paces). The tool can say the can was filled. It cannot say whether
  you sighed at the top.
- **The ear gate, still.** Five new voices in `out/sound/` — a clock
  set and the bell, a can filled, a can emptied into a tank, an oar
  stood against wood, a plate cleared. Unheard, like the ninety-five
  before.
- **Sheet 18 §0.** The fifteen-second rule's measure is still yours.
- **Sheets 16 to 20.** Still open, all five.
