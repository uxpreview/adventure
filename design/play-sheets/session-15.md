# PLAY SHEET — Session 15, THE VERBS AND THE LAW

*Ten minutes. The play gate is yours (`QUALITY-BAR.md` §2): no tool in
this repository can say whether any of this is fun, and this session
did not run the gate. It hands you the script.*

**The build:** the branch `claude/session-15-frx4ov` — the pull request's
preview deployment, or `npm run dev` on the branch. Once merged it is
[adventure.ryankm.com](https://adventure.ryankm.com). **Play on a fresh
page** (SET OUT, not KEEP WALKING): a save from Session 14 works, but
the cart, the stone and the king's card are all first-time things.

**The one key.** `E` (or `Space`, `Enter`, or tap the prompt) still
looks. It also touches, picks up, throws and sits now, and the prompt
tells you which. If you never touch anything, nothing has changed.

---

## 1. Wake. (0:00)

You are at the crossroads. Walk north-west to the well — thirty
seconds. The prompt used to say LOOK DOWN THE WELL; it says **SHOUT
DOWN THE WELL**.

**Press it.** Then wait. It is meant to take too long. Watch the
swallows while you wait.

*Ask:* did you wait, or did you walk off? Was the answer worth the
wait? Press it again — it is meant to be repeatable. Is it?

## 2. The stone. (1:00)

Walk east along the long fence to the field gate, past the person
leaning on it. There is a **stone** by the gate, propping it. The
prompt says PICK UP THE STONE.

**Press it.** It is in your hand. Walk with it. Standing still the
prompt says PUT DOWN THE STONE; walk and it says THROW THE STONE.
**Throw it a few times.** Throw it into the river at Riverbend (south-
east). Throw it from a run and from a standstill.

*Ask:* does it feel like throwing, or like a menu? Is the arc right?
Did you want a second stone? (There is deliberately not one. Tell me if
that is wrong.)

Then carry it back to the well and throw it down the well from the
path, at a walk, from outside the well's reach — the prompt will say
THROW while you are moving. It is gone. Tomorrow morning it is back by
the gate.

## 3. The cart. (3:00)

Back to the field gate. The **hay cart** is just beyond it. Walk to its
side; the prompt says **PUSH THE CART**.

**Push it east.** Keep pushing. Walk after it and push again — it rolls
about five units a shove and slows. **Push it all the way to the edge
of the Common** (east, toward the Downs — about forty units, eight or
nine shoves). It stops two units short of the border and will not cross.

*Ask:* is it satisfying, or a chore? Does five units a shove feel
right, or should one shove roll it further? Is the stop at the border
a wall or a rule? **Reload the page** — it is where you left it.

## 4. The swing. (5:00)

Walk west to the arguing oaks. Under the leaning oak the prompt says
**SIT IN THE SWING** (the oaks' own note is a few strides further,
TAKE A SIDE).

**Sit.** The camera should not move at all. The day runs six times
faster while you sit. Sit until the light changes. Press any direction
to stand.

*Ask:* did the camera hold still, and did that feel right or dead? Is
six times too fast, too slow?

## 5. The toppled king. (6:30)

North, up the king's road, through Brim, up the banner avenue, through
the gatehouse, into the bailey. The fallen statue on the left. The
prompt says **SET YOUR SHOULDER TO HIM**.

**Press it.** A card. Read it. Two doors under it. **Both are visible
before you choose.** Choose whichever you like — `1` or `2`, or tap.

- If you put him back: he stands. Walk back down the avenue: every
  banner is down and there is a bare pole under each. The wind's crack
  has stopped. The moat pool at the ridge's west foot, which had this
  week's red in it, is clear. Walk away and come back — it stays.
  Reload — it stays.
- If you leave him: nothing changes. Come back to the plinth: the card
  is not offered again. That was also a choice.

*Ask:* did you know what each door would cost before you chose? Does
the card feel like a card in this world, or like a UI from another
game? Did you want a third option? **Which did you choose, and why?**
Nothing in the game says which was right. Tell me if it should.

## 6. Be in the Downs at dawn. (9:00)

The sheep move at first light. From the crossroads go east along the
east road, over the crease, over the bridge, and turn south down to the
sunken lane between two hedges — THE DROVE, at about (101, 100). Get
there before **a quarter to six** on the day clock. (The day is forty
minutes; if it is the wrong hour, sit somewhere — the swing, the king —
and let it run.)

At a quarter to six the flock sets out from the fold at the lane's
south end, walks north up the lane past you, through the gate, and
into the field beyond, where it stands all day. **Two of them do not
move.** At about twenty past seven in the evening they come home.

It happens whether or not you are there: arrive at noon and the lane
is nearly empty and the field is full.

*Ask:* did you see it, or did you have to be told? Is it a thing you
would tell somebody about? Was getting there in time a chore?

## What I could not judge, and am not claiming

- Whether any of it is **fun**. That is the gate, and it is yours.
- Whether the well's delay is too long or exactly right.
- Whether five units a shove and six times the clock are the numbers.
- Whether the choice card's tone is right: it is the plinth's note with
  two doors under it, and a card is the first thing in this game that
  asks you something.
- **The ear gate, still.** Five new sounds are in `out/sound/` — the
  shout, the answer, the wheels, the stone on the page and in the water
  — unheard, like the fifty-three before them.
- **The feel gate, still**, plus one new state: sitting.

Everything above is measured where it can be (`tools/check-verbs.mjs`)
and photographed both ways (`tools/shoot-session15.mjs`). None of that
is a verdict.
