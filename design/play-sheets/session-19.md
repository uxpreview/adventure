## 1. The longship, from the promenade. (`?hour=6.6`, 0:00)

Go to the coast: west from the crossroads to the boardwalk, then north
up the promenade to its end and on across the bight's sand. **Out to
the left, a longship is coming in** — sail set, oars out, seven
shields along her. Keep walking north toward the point. By seven she
is beached at the foot of the cliff, sail furled. Stand on the sand
near her. **They roar at you.** They will go on roaring, every ten
seconds or so, for as long as you stand on the sand.

*Ask:* the first time — was it a threat? Did you step back? And the
third roar — was it funny yet? (It is meant to be a threat first and a
joke after, and the joke is that they cannot land: the berth is the
Wide Blue's and the sand is yours, and nothing will ever say so.)

## 2. The horn. (`?hour=12`, 2:00)

Up the cut to the point. Beside the cairn there is **a horn on a
stone**. BLOW THE HORN. Wait a beat and a half.

*Ask:* did you laugh? Did you blow it again? A third time? (It is a
toy: no score, no end, and they answer every time, from wherever they
are — at noon from the mark, which is a long way off.)

## 3. Noon on the bar. (`?hour=12.3`, 3:00)

Walk the sandbar out toward the mark. **The longship is in the race**,
going round the buoy with the fleet under a striped sail. On the bar's
root, where the sand starts, **a punt is drawn up and somebody is
mending something beside it** in the morning; at noon they are gone,
and if you look out at the mark there is a small boat beside the bell.
Pick up **the stone on the crest** and throw it at the water at a run.

*Ask:* did it skip? How many times did you throw it? (Three skips and
in. The morning puts it back.)

## 4. The surfers. (`?hour=6.1`, 4:30)

North of the huts, on the shelf of sand under the point: **a van, a
rack, a wetsuit on a line.** At first light two people come out of the
van, walk down to the water's edge, stand and look at it, walk back,
and sit on the step. **Read the sticker.** Then go down the beach to
the boardwalk: **a board is lying on the wrack**. Pick it up. Carry it
back to the rack and put it down beside it.

*Ask:* did you know what to do with the board before the rack told
you? Did carrying it feel like a job or a joke? Was the sticker worth
reading twice?

## 5. Shelter Cove and Pye. (`?hour=6.8`, 6:00)

Over the point and down into the cove. **A man is rowing a line of
seven pots**, pausing at each. By eight he is on the sand at the pots,
mending; in the afternoon he sits on the boat's gunwale. Walk up to the
pots. COUNT THE POTS. **If you have been to the mark** (the bell buoy
out in the Wide Blue — its note names it), the pots are a card with two
doors: tell him the mark's name, or haul the pots. **Take one.** Come
back the next morning.

*Ask:* which door, and why? Did the cost read as a cost? (One of them
puts an eighth pot further out than he will ever row. The other empties
the line and the shape of his day with it. Nothing says which was
right, and the 8:15 will remember.)

## 6. Wick at dawn. (`?hour=5.6`, 7:30)

The avenue up to Greyweather, from Brim's north gate. **Halfway up, on
the verge, an old man is resting with a rolled banner across his
knees.** Wait. He gets up, goes to the bottom pole, changes the banner,
and goes down to the moat pool with it. **If you have read the square
in Brim** (the red cloth on the near stall), walk up the avenue and
look at the gate: there is a **fifth banner** on a pole of its own, in
Brim's colours. At the gate, RATTLE THE PORTCULLIS.

*Ask:* did Wick read as a person with a job, or as furniture that
moves? Did the fifth banner mean anything, standing there? (It is the
first time two lands are in one frame and neither crossed anything.)
And the keep — stand in the bailey: is it a place now, or still cards
on grey ground?

## 7. The monsters. (`?day=1&hour=12`, then `?day=1&hour=22.8`, 9:00)

The moat pool, on the second day: **the water is red.** It was not
yesterday. Read the note. Come back after dark and stand by the reeds:
something moves in the water, once, and the reeds shiver. Then the
deep pines at night (`?hour=23`): walk into the stand north-east of
the tarn and stand still among the trunks for ten seconds. **Something
is drawn at the edge of the frame. Walk toward it.**

*Ask:* did the red frighten you or amuse you? Did the shape in the
pines make you turn round in your chair? Did you want an explanation,
and would one have helped? (There is none, anywhere, ever. And the
morning after the thing under the Wide Blue surfaces — every third
dusk — the seals do not haul out on the bar, and nothing says why.)

## What moved, and what I could not judge

Thirty-seven of ninety-two protected frames moved past the threshold,
and every one has something this session built standing in it: the
castle's three framings at both hours (the keep's stone, the yard, the
portcullis, the fifth banner), the tide line and the boardwalk (the
van, the board on the wrack, the jetty lamp), the point and the cut
(the horn), the sandbar (Wren at the mark at noon, the longship beached
at dusk), the avenue's foot (the portcullis in the far arch), and the
crossroads and the well (the labels, moved a line-height up by the QA
pass). **The largest is Brim's street shot on a phone, at ninety-six
per cent, and it is the QA pass working**: the base frame was shot
through the south gate's own drawing, a brown wash across the whole
picture, and now the gate fades when the lens is inside it. `SESSIONS.md`
has every number and the evidence is a `SAVE=1` run away.

- Whether any of it is **fun**. That is the gate, and it is yours.
- **Whether the roar is a threat the first time.** The tool can say
  the longship never lands. It cannot say whether you flinched.
- **The three named people at the dusk grade** are the folk's size and
  half of them goes into the sepia. If you cannot find Wick on the
  avenue at 5.6, say so and they get bigger.
- **The ear gate, still.** Six new voices in `out/sound/` — the roar,
  the horn, the portcullis, a board set in a rack, the moat at night,
  a stone skipping. Unheard, like the eighty before.
- **Sheet 18 §0.** The fifteen-second rule's measure is still yours.
