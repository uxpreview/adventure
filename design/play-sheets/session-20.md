## 1. The low dog and the ball. (`?hour=12`, 0:00)

Maple Court, the green (south-east of the court, over the river
bridge). **A long dog in a neckerchief comes to you** as soon as you
are on the grass. There is a ball on the lawn. PICK UP THE BALL, run,
and press the key: **a throw at a run is a kick.** Watch what the dog
does. Then walk off the green, west, toward the court.

*Ask:* did it feel like a dog? Did you kick it twice? Three times?
(It brings the ball back and does not stop. And it stops dead at the
edge of the green and sits and looks after you — the goat's rule at
the size of a lawn. Nothing says why.)

## 2. The visitors, by day. (`?hour=10`, 2:00)

The Bleach Flats, the Pale (west of the oasis, across the pan).
**Something has burned a ruled pattern into the ground**, and three of
your one-eyed things are standing in it. Walk up to the big one.
**POKE IT.** Again.

*Ask:* did you laugh? Is it absurd enough by day? Read THE PALE's
note: does it explain anything? (It must not.)

## 3. The visitors, by night. (`?hour=23`, 3:30)

Same place, after dark. **The bodies are gone. There are three lights
over the pan.** Walk toward one.

*Ask:* did it come toward you? Did you stop walking? (They come to
twelve units and no nearer, and never leave the pan, and there is a
hum under the grit. This is the same drawing as §2 with the day taken
off it. Was it frightening, or only dark?)

## 4. The barista, and the dog in the bow tie. (`?hour=12.2`, 5:00)

Greyline City, the junction, the south-west corner. **A coffee cart,
a person behind it, and your dog sat beside it in its bow tie.** READ
THE CUPS. Wait for the hour to turn (the clock runs; or `?hour=12.99`
and stand there): **a name is called out**, and a cup goes down.

*Ask:* did you recognise the names? When did it stop being funny?
(Eleven of them cannot come and collect. The eleventh cup has nothing
written on it. Nothing says so.) Then `?hour=6.9`, stand by the
junction and look north: **the barista and the dog walk in from the
north end, and the dog leaves paw prints.** Are they yours or its?

## 5. The bin. (`?hour=12`, 6:30)

North-east corner of the junction: **a wheelie bin on its side.**
RIGHT THE BIN. Then PUSH THE BIN, toward the crossing, twice.

*Ask:* did the pigeons going up feel like the city noticing? Is a bin
you can shove a toy, or a chore? (Goat Simulator is on the list.)

## 6. The pavement's two doors. (`?hour=12`, 7:00)

Stand on the pavement near the man, but not within arm's reach. LOOK
DOWN. Read it. Press again: **a card, two doors.** Walk round, like
everyone. Then walk past him three times without stopping.

*Ask:* which door, and why? Did the third pass feel like anything?
(A lane appears in the stone, nearer him than the others, and it is
yours. He is still standing. If you took the other door, stand still
four seconds; that is Session 13's, unchanged.)

## 7. The studio, the stickies, the chair. (`?hour=8.1`, 8:00)

The Cubicle Mile, the atrium's forecourt. **A persona on an easel
called DENNIS. A journey map.** READ THE PERSONA. Then the 8:15 stop:
**somebody with a clipboard is standing beside Dennis while he reads
the board.** Come back at `?hour=16.2`: **the shelter's glass is
covered in sticky notes.** PEEL ONE OFF.

*Ask:* is it accurate? Is it too accurate? (It is your field, and
the persona says *it says 8:15*.) Then the road, by the atrium: **an
office chair.** Face along the road, SIT ON THE CHAIR.

*Ask:* did you say anything out loud? Did you go and get it and do it
again the other way? (It stops at a building, the shelter, the doors,
or the mile's edge, and stays there.)

## 8. The square flock. (`?hour=12`, 9:00)

North of the atrium, the overflow car park. **Six sheep, one to a
bay, square.** Walk into them.

*Ask:* did you check whether they were all sheep? (One has curled
horns. One has long horns and a face in the middle of the square and
does not move for anybody. Read THE OVERFLOW's note.)

## 9. Val's two doors. (`?hour=12`, then `?hour=19.6&day=3`, 9:30)

With the castle's name (`?hour=12` and go to Greyweather's gate first,
or trust the sheet): the three chairs in Maple Court. TELL HER WHAT
YOU SAW. **Turn her light off.** Come back at dusk on day three.

*Ask:* did the dark street read as your doing? (One house a day. Val
was holding the line. The other door cuts the gap, and that one is
the wait's answer.)
