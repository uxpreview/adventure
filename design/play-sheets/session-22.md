## 0. The rain. (`?hour=14.3`, 0:00)

Stand anywhere on the Common with the sound on. It is raining, and the
rain is not the thing you called horrible: it is drops now, on the
page, with a low wash under it when it comes down hard, and every few
seconds one drop you can pick out. Wait two minutes and it eases off;
the drops come further apart before the patter goes.

*Ask:* does it sound like rain on paper, or rain on a roof? Is the
wash under a downpour too much? (The storm is on the second night,
`?hour=22.8` after a day has passed, with the thunder over it.) The
three weights are also in `out/sound/weather-rain-*.wav` and
`weather-storm.wav` if you run `node tools/render-wavs.mjs`.

## 1. The crown. (`?hour=12`, 1:30)

Greyweather, the bailey, the toppled king. Take either door at the
plinth — put him back, or leave him. Then walk to the head end of him:
**TAKE HIS CROWN.** It is on you. Look up: there is a third button in
the corner now, beside `map` and `sound`, and it says *wearing: the
crown*. Press it. Press it again.

*Ask:* did you want it? Does it read as a crown at the walker's size,
on a phone? Read the plinth afterwards: *his head is bare. nobody has
mentioned it.* Is that the right amount of nothing? (He is drawn
crownless from now on, lying or standing, in every save.)

## 2. The hat. (`?hour=7.3`, 3:00)

Longshore, the coast road east of the boardwalk. At twenty to eight a
hat comes along the road from the west at seven units a second — faster
than you — and stops at the Common's border. Stand in its way and press
when it says **CATCH THE HAT.** Miss it, and it lies at the border until
eleven: **PICK UP THE HAT** is the easy way.

*Ask:* did you try to catch it? How many runs did it take? (Caught or
picked up, it never runs the coast road again. That encounter is gone
from your world for good, and you are wearing it.)

## 3. The lanyard and the helm. (`?hour=12`, 4:30)

The Cubicle Mile, the atrium: with the board decided (wiped, or the
corner pressed), the fourth lanyard on the persona's easel says
VISITOR, and **TAKE THE SPARE LANYARD** is yours. It hangs at the neck.
The Wide Blue: if you have called the finish at Wren's punt, walk the
sand under the Holdfast the next morning. Something is lying on the
foreshore below the point. **PICK UP THE HELM.** Read the longship's
note afterwards.

*Ask:* four things, four lands, four keys — emotional, comedy, deadpan,
threat-then-comedy. Which one did you put on and leave on? Is a helm
with horns a joke you want, given the crew are drawn without them?

## 4. How deep. (`?hour=12`, 6:00)

Splitrock, the floor, halfway up the cut. A man with a coil of line.
**ASK WHAT HE MEASURED.** Ten point eight, lip to floor, twice. Now the
Flats: on the south bank of the oasis, **TRY THE LINE IN IT.** Now the
Wide Blue, out on the bar at THE LONG WATER: **PAY OUT THE LINE.** Then
back to him: **TELL HIM WHAT HAPPENED TO THE LINE.**

*Ask:* three lands for one chalk mark. Was the walk worth the mark?
Did you look for what was written beside it? (Nothing is. Nobody in
this world will ever know how deep the sea is, and the note does not
say either.)

## 5. The timetable means nothing until the names do. (7:30)

The Cubicle Mile's stop, on a fresh page, before you have been
anywhere else: **CHECK THE TIMETABLE.** *Twelve names down the left,
most of them just words.* Go and stand in five more lands and come
back. The same board reads differently, and this time it hands you the
twelfth piece.

*Ask:* did you notice the board had changed? Should you? (This is
`critique-story-2`'s standing recommendation, three sessions old, built:
the list resolves Dennis's wait only once six of the twelve names are
yours, which the map already draws.)

## 6. The keys. (8:30)

Read THE WOOD ROAD in the Penwood, RIVERBEND on the Common, THE MOUTH
in Splitrock, WHERE THE ROAD STOPS in the Flats. Each was re-read in
its land's key — frightening, frightening, awe, awe — and rewritten
where it was winking at the paper instead.

*Ask:* does the Penwood feel colder at the wood road than it did? Does
the Common's river bend make you look at the water?

Four lands re-opened for the worn things — GREYWEATHER, LONGSHORE, THE
WIDE BLUE, THE CUBICLE MILE — and SPLITROCK, THE FLATS and THE WIDE
BLUE for the stranger. Nothing new was placed inside a protected
framing on a fresh page: the crown, the lanyard and the helm exist only
after a door, the hat's place is where the hat already was, Odd stands
on the floor at (309, −204), south of THE OVERLOOK's frame.

- **Whether the rain is good.** `check-audio` §9 can prove it is not a
  hiss — a crest of five to eleven times over its average where a

  it. The first thing in the ear pack you have actually judged, and the
  judgement was one word; this is the answer to it.

  is on. It cannot say whether you smiled.
- **The ear gate, still.** Six new voices (a crown lifted, a hat caught,
  a lanyard's clip, a helm off wet sand, a line paid out, chalk cut)
  and the three rain files. Unheard, like the hundred before.
