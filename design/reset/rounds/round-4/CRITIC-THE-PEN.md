The pen has a real hand: one lettering from the title through the note card, the map footer, the notebook tabs and every FOUND line; the belfry run (071–077) with rain streaks, a rose-grey wash, amber windows and one red scarf on a black stick figure is the best still I have seen from a zero-asset build, and the fence-and-orange-flowers frames (031–033) hold up beside A Short Hike. But a third of the strip does not hold up. Whenever the figure moves it is drawn as a head on a single line with no legs (042, 046–048, 059, 072, 074, 076); the first act is smothered by translucent olive hedge blobs that sit on top of Nell, the gate and the bull (004–020) so the bull is a grey smear with horns; the town wall is eighty seconds of flat grey rectangles; map labels pile into one smudge (021, 038, 049, 066); speech bubbles print over the map/notebook buttons (068–070). On sound the evidence is nil: a "sound: on" toggle, and a player who never mentions hearing a footstep, a whistle or the bell the whole ending hangs on. Frame rate is unshown, and the player did not complain. Goose Game and A Short Hike never lose the lead's legs and their audio is half their beauty; this one is beautiful at dusk and unfinished at walking pace.
