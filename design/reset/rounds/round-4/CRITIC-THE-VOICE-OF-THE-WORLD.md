But the pillar promises every action answered within a second, and the strip shows the world going mute: E on TALK TO NELL does nothing twice (frames 8–9, the very first conversation); H whistles the horse to the wrong side of the fence with no line (frame 40); and frames 41–57, a fifth of the session, are one figure running along an identical grey wall with a single hint that pointed the wrong way. Then the square (frames 62–65) holds six or seven drawn people and only Marget has a name or a sentence. Stardew's Pelican Town is blander line for line, but every figure in it answers when you walk up. In ten minutes I met two people who talk. The voice is lovely; it is not yet reliable.
