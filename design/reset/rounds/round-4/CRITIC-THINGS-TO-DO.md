On THINGS TO DO this strip is thin. Across 77 frames I count two jobs with names (NELL — GET ON THE HORSE / LEAD THE BULL THROUGH THE GATE; MARGET — WAIT AT THE BELFRY TILL THE LAMPS COME ON / SETTLE THE HOUR), one counter that ticks (1 OF 12 KEPT, 2 OF 12 KEPT, "2 of 12 lands walked"), zero toys with a score, and one monster that chases: the bull, which flattens the player twice in frames 15–18 and is stood still for good by frame 30. After that nothing on screen wants anything from the player. Frames 41–57 are a stick figure jogging along a blank grey wall with no prompt at all; frames 70–72 are the player sitting on a bench for 150 game-seconds. The square in frame 62 has seven people, a bird, a fountain and bunting, and exactly one of them, Marget, can be talked to; nothing can be pushed, carried, or knocked over. The notebook's own list page reads "the first page is stuck to the cover. it can wait." Untitled Goose Game hands you the list in the first minute, every rake, radio and boot in the garden is a toy whether it is listed or not, and the groundskeeper chases you the whole level while you steal his hat between items. Here the toy count is zero and the chase lasts thirty seconds.
