On the frames alone, INKLANDS has one land that is a yard and one that is nearly a place. The Common (frames 2–31) is a bench, a washing line, a gate and a well under a castle skyline that sits like a painted flat; the only thing that moves in it is the bull, which is good — it fills the bottom of frames 15, 17 and 20. Then the horse: earned at frame 30, whistled at 39, standing on the wrong side of a fence by 40 and never crossing it — "mounts that go anywhere" fails in the first minute of owning one. Frames 41–57 are the worst case: seventeen frames of cream sky, flat grass and grey wall with nothing alive but Wren's footprints; the player calls it the stretch where the game went silent. Brim's square (62–65) has stalls, bunting, a bird and half a dozen figures, and the lamp-lit dusk (73–77) is the one moment the world breathes. A Short Hike's island is smaller than this sheet, but a bird, a runner, a jumping fish or a kid with a stick turns up every ten seconds, and the glide carries you over any fence. The grass never moves, the sky is empty in all 77 frames, and the mount stops at the first rail.
