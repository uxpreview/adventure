A Short Hike's first ten minutes never lets you stand still not knowing: Aunt May names the peak, the first feather is on the path, every creature with a face answers, and the island walks you uphill. This strip shows the opposite. Frame 2 says READ THE SIGNPOST and never names a key; frames 4–7 are the same stuck map while Nell shouts "RUN. THE GATE. NOW." at a gate (frames 12–14) that does nothing when you reach it, and the real exit, the stile in frame 17, is found by wandering, not by teaching. The gateway figure with the horse and cart (28–29) and the street stranger (32–33) are furniture, indistinguishable from Marget until her prompt appears. Marget then assigns READ THE MARKET CROSS (frame 37) and the next twenty-eight frames (45–65) are the player pressed against house walls, hunting an object neither map nor square marks; frame 54's WAIT FOR THE BELL only re-opens a note. What works is real: the fence's "the stile is for people who have met it," the fountain's "next week for a long time," region names fading in, footprints inking onto the map. But A Short Hike would have had this player talking to five islanders and holding a feather by now; here the clearest verb in the log is "retrace."
