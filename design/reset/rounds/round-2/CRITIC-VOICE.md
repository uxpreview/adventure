For seventy seconds INKLANDS talks better than most shipped games: Nell's shout-line rewrites itself at every step (RUN → READ THE SIGNPOST → BRING THE FOURTH NAME BACK), the signpost card admits "8:15, which is not a place," and the choice at the field gate is actually read back — the same card that said "it has been almost loaded for years" becomes "she looks at it the way you look at a bag by the door," the cap lands on the player's head as a HUD chip, and Nell and her cart are physically gone from the gate and standing in Brim's square a minute later. That is a New Vegas readback in miniature, and I saw it in the frames. But the pillar is the voice of a *world*, and after frame 26 the world goes mute. One person spoke in ten minutes; Marget is behind a wall that says nothing when you push it forty times (frames 41–45), the two figures in the Home Field never look up and never speak, Joan Harrow is a name in the tracker, and the stray label "look" floats unexplained. Worse, every one of Nell's bubbles is drawn over the player's head (frames 9, 10, 29–34), so her voice reads as mine. Stardew has Pierre, Robin and Lewis greeting you by day three; here three of four named people are silent, and the town is a locked drawing.
