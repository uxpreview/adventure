On scale, INKLANDS earns something: Brim's wall and skyline sit on the horizon in nearly every meadow frame (003–028) the way Hawk Peak does in A Short Hike, the map's "N of 12 lands walked" counter and the region banners ("the walled town / THE KINGDOM OF BRIM", "farm country / THE HARROW DOWNS") make distance legible, and the ink footprints and hoofprints (016, 035) leave a record of where you have been. On inhabitation it loses. In ten minutes the player met one person who does anything, Nell, and her relocation with the cart to Brim square (036–037) is the single best moment in the strip. Everyone else is scenery: the two field hands in the Home Field (051–054) never look up, E only reads the field card, and the prose even excuses it. The same three animal scribbles recur from the common to the Downs. Brim, the one town, is flat grey billboards that slide over the player (041–045), a stray floating "look" label, and an invisible line at z=-12 that the player pushed against for forty seconds while the cart sat visible beyond it. A Short Hike would, by this point, have had a dozen birds walking, talking, racing you, and a summit you could actually climb toward.
