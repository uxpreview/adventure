# INKLANDS — cold play report (round 2)

Played from the title screen. I stopped at gameSec ~245, not 600: I ran out of my ~90-command budget, mostly because I burned a lot of commands trying to get into Brim and trying to get onto the horse. Everything below is what actually happened.

## 1. First sixty seconds

The title screen said **INKLANDS — "a world in one sheet — twelve lands, one pen"**, over a pencil-and-wash drawing of a meadow, a well, a signpost, a cart, and a big castle wall behind. One button: **set out**. I clicked it.

The screen faded in from white for about three seconds. Then a line appeared top-left: **NELL — RUN. THE GATE IS WEST**, a speech bubble on the left said **RUN. THE GATE. NOW.**, and the bottom of the screen said **hold shift to run**. A big beetle/cow thing was standing by the cart next to me. I held shift+A and ran left. The "hold shift to run" hint vanished as soon as I ran. In about ten seconds I reached **THE FIELD GATE**, a prompt said **TALK TO NELL**, I pressed E, and Nell said: "Three names on that signpost I could go to tomorrow. It's the fourth I want. THE CROSSROADS — it's on your map." Then **PINNED: THE CROSSROADS / M FOR THE MAP**. I opened the map, saw the Crossroads a stone's throw north, walked there, read the signpost (BRIM, THE SEA, DOWNS, and a fourth that says **8:15**), got told to take the south road to Maple Court and read a milestone, did that, and was running back to Nell at the 60-second mark.

**At gameSec 60, can I say what this game wants me to do?** Yes: it wants me to walk around a hand-drawn world, read the things written on it (signposts, milestones, gates), carry what I read back to people, and slowly work out what "8:15" is. The notebook line "THE 8:15 WILL STOP FOR 0 OF 12" strongly suggests the long goal is to get all twelve lands ready for something (a train? a coach?) that comes at 8:15. **Sureness: 8/10.** I'm sure about the moment-to-moment loop; I'm only guessing about what the 8:15 is.

## 2. What I understood

- **World.** A single sheet-of-paper world drawn in ink and wash. Regions I saw: **the common / meadow** (where you wake), **Maple Court** ("the neighborhood", south), **the Kingdom of Brim** ("the walled town", north), **the Harrow Downs** ("farm country", east). The map's footer counts "N of 12 lands walked" and "strides of ink". Learned by: shown (region banners fade in when you cross a border; the map fills in names after you hear of them).
- **Goal.** Short term: Nell's errand ("THE FOURTH NAME") — told directly by Nell and by the notebook's checklist. Long term: "THE 8:15 WILL STOP FOR 1 OF 12" — shown in the notebook, never explained; I guessed. After Nell's job, a bubble told me there are three more people with things to do: Marget in Brim's square, Joan Harrow on the Downs, Val at the top of Maple Court. Told.
- **Controls.** WASD moves; shift runs (told, bottom-of-screen hint). E talks/reads/mounts (guessed, it's the obvious key; the prompt text never said which key). M map, N notebook (told). Mouse-drag to look, R to recentre (told, after the first job). H whistles the horse (told). Space seemed to mount the horse once, by accident (guessed). Clicking "put it back" closes a reading card (shown).
- **Reading.** Anything named (signpost, milestone, gate, field) gives a little cream card of prose when you press E near it. Shown.
- **Horse.** Horses wander the meadow; get close and a **GET ON THE HORSE** prompt appears; on it you go about twice as fast; the horse follows you around afterwards. Shown/guessed.

## 4. What reacted

Things that answered me:
- The "hold shift to run" hint disappeared the moment I ran. Good.
- Nell's shout line at top-left updated at every step of the job (RUN → READ THE SIGNPOST → TAKE THE SOUTH ROAD → READ THE MILESTONE → BRING THE FOURTH NAME BACK). Her speech bubbles kept coming as I walked away ("Read it. Bring me the fourth name and I'll owe you." / "8:15. That's not a place. That's a time.").
- Every named thing I pressed E at gave a card of prose. All of it was good writing.
- The notebook ticked checkboxes; the map grew names as I "heard of" places and marked "you" and the pin.
- Region banners ("the neighborhood / MAPLE COURT", "the walled town / THE KINGDOM OF BRIM", "farm country / THE HARROW DOWNS") fade in on crossing a border, with an "OVER THE BORDER" toast.
- I leave ink footprints. When I'm on the horse the footprints become hoofprints.
- A horse followed me from the crossroads down to Maple Court and back on its own, and the world put it on a hitching rope near a post at the crossroads.
- The choice at the gate had a real consequence: Nell and the cart actually left, and I later found them at Brim's south gate and then inside the square. The cap appeared on my character and a "wearing: nell's cap" chip appeared in the HUD.
- Pressing E with no prompt near the horse gave a hint ("Closer, and it says GET ON THE HORSE"). Pressing E with no prompt at the south gate read the gate card anyway.
- Mouse-drag turned the camera; R recentred it.
- On the horse, a WHOA prompt appeared at the gate and I went about 10 units/sec instead of 4.5.

Things where I did something and nothing happened:
- Running north into Brim from z≈-12: nothing, for forty game-seconds, from three x positions, mounted and on foot, with space. No message, no bump, no "you can't". Just the number not changing.
- Space: I never learned what it does. Once it seemed to remount me; otherwise nothing.
- Chasing wandering horses (gameSec 33–39, 165–172): they drift and the prompt only appears in a very narrow band. Standing visually on top of the horse gives nothing.
- Walking into hay sheaves in the Home Field: silent stop.
- A stray word "look" appeared in the HUD text list near the horse at Brim and in the Downs, with no explanation. It was rendered as a floating label off to the right. I don't know what it is.
- The notebook showed "READ THE SIGNPOST AT THE CROSSROADS" unchecked immediately after I'd read it; it ticked a couple of seconds later once I'd closed the book.
- Nell's speech bubble is drawn over *my* head, not hers, when she calls after me. It reads as if I'm saying her lines.

## 5. What I wanted to do next and could not

- **Get into Brim.** This is the big one. The whole thread of the story (the arrow points north, up the king's road, through Brim) and the next named person (Marget in Brim's square) are behind that wall, Nell's cart went through, the gate card says the portcullis is up, and I could see a person and the cart in the square — and I could not step past the line. I spent ~25 commands on it.
- **Talk to Joan Harrow.** I was in her field, two figures were working in it, and E only ever read the field card. Maybe she's not one of those figures, maybe she's off to the side; I ran out of commands.
- **Ride the horse reliably.** Whistling works, but the mount prompt band is so narrow that finding it costs five or six moves each time.
- **Know what space and "look" do.**
- **See what "KEEP IT, AND PUSH THE CART YOURSELF" would have done.** I'd like a second save.

## 6. Best and worst moment

**Best:** Coming back up the road to Nell, getting the choice card, telling her the name, and then reading "she looks at it the way you look at a bag by the door" — and then, a minute later, finding her and the cart actually gone from the gate and waiting at Brim. The world remembered. Also: the cap on my head. Tiny thing, huge.

**Worst:** Standing in the gate passage of Brim, portcullis up, Nell's cart visible twenty yards ahead, pushing W for five seconds and watching z sit at -12.9. No message, no reason. It broke the spell more than anything else, because everything up to then had answered me.

## 7. To a friend

It's a walk-and-read game drawn like a sketchbook: you cross a paper world of twelve little lands, press E on signposts and gates to read a paragraph of very good prose, and carry names and errands between a handful of quiet people while a mystery about "the 8:15" ticks up in your notebook. It feels like A Short Hike's gentleness with Wind Waker's map-filling, and a bit of Kentucky Route Zero in the writing.

Three things I want to do next time:
1. Get past Brim's south gate (try approaching from the map's dotted road exactly, or at a different time of day) and find Marget in the square.
2. Find Val at the top of Maple Court and see what "the 8:15 will stop for N of 12" means when a second land is done.
3. Reload and choose "push the cart yourself" to see where you can push it and what "it stays" means.

## 8. Scores

- **Understandable: 7/10.** The errand loop, the map, and the notebook teach themselves. Loses points for the silent wall at Brim, the invisible mount band on the horse, and the unexplained "look" label.
- **Alive: 7/10.** Horses follow you, Nell moves house when you finish her job, cards react to E without prompts, region banners, footprints. Loses points because nobody in the Home Field would talk to me and the town I could see was unreachable.
- **Fun: 6/10.** The first 75 seconds were a small, complete, satisfying story. The next 170 were mostly me pushing against things.
- **Beautiful: 9/10.** The ink-and-wash look, the hand-lettered cards, the little cap, the way the map is drawn. I'd screenshot this a lot.
