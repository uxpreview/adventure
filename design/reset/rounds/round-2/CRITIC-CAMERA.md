INKLANDS gets the first half of the pillar almost by accident: from frame 3 on, Brim's towers and wall sit on the horizon in nearly every shot, and the player did exactly what Breath of the Wild trains you to do, ran at the thing on the skyline (frames 27–29, 36–45), and the thing refused them, a silent z=-12 line with the camera clipped inside translucent grey building-slabs (43, 45) so you cannot even see what is stopping you. The Plateau works because the horizon is a promise the camera keeps; here it is a drawing. Beyond that the camera is a fixed shoulder-follow that never moves for the world: crossing into MAPLE COURT (18) or THE HARROW DOWNS (49) is a text banner over identical ground, no crane, no pull-back, no vista. Free-look is withheld until frame 25 ("drag the mouse to look around, R to recentre"), and when used (26) it reveals a house and Nell, then is never used again. Occluders are handled by painting dark hexagonal blobs over the protagonist (5, 6, 22, 24, 31–35, 51–53) rather than dollying in, so the frames where something is near you are the ugliest. The one earned camera moment is the hill roll into the Downs (47).
