For seventy-five seconds INKLANDS out-teaches A Short Hike. Frame 3 is a shout ("RUN. THE GATE. NOW."), a "hold shift to run" hint that dies the moment you obey it, and a west that is actually west; frames 6–24 chain gate, "M FOR THE MAP", signpost, milestone, and back to a real choice card, and the cap appears on the figure's head in frame 26. That is teaching by playing, the thing the reference does with Aunt May and the first feather. But A Short Hike's first ten minutes never stop answering: every cliff is climbable, every stranger talks, no visible place is sealed. Here, frames 36–46 are eleven near-identical shots of a capped rider parked against grey blocks under a floating "GET OFF", Nell's cart visible through the arch, with no bump, no line, no card refusing the player; the report clocks forty game-seconds of pushing W at z=-12.9. Then the Downs: sheaves that stop you silently, two workers who will not speak, and a stray "look" label hanging in the right margin (frames 44, 52, 53). The player's own arc, "Fun 6/10, the next 170 seconds were me pushing against things", is the pillar verdict. A first hour is judged by whether minute two still opens doors; this one shut them.
