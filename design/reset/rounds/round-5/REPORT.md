# INKLANDS — cold player report, round 5 (2026-09-17)

*Build `ec600b2` (the three verbs). Desktop, from the title, no
parameters. The sub-agent could not write files; this is its returned
report, saved by the session. It stopped at gameSec ~220 of 600, out of
commands; about 140 of those seconds went into one gate. It could not
hear anything.*

## 1. First sixty seconds

The title said **INKLANDS — "a world in one sheet — twelve lands, one pen"** over a pencil-and-wash drawing of a common, with one button: **set out**. I clicked it.

I was sat on a bench. Big soft letters said "where you woke — THE COMMON". Bottom right said **STAND UP**, with no key named. I held W and stood up. A woman off to the right said "Oh. You're back." Two toasts: FOUND: THE BENCH and FOUND: THE NOTE.

I walked toward a scrap of paper. **"wasd to walk · drag to look · E to act"** appeared and the prompt changed to READ THE NOTE. The note read: *"back in an hour. — you. pinned to the bench with a tack, in your own handwriting, and it has been rained on more than once. nobody has taken it down."* I loved that immediately.

I walked to the woman (NELL, at the washing line). She had already changed her line because I'd read the note: "You said you'd only be gone an hour." I pressed E to talk. Her lines arrive a second or so after the press, so my first two presses felt dead. Then "Don't look at me like that." and "Three years, and you just stand there."

Then a card: **"you can't remember your name. what will you go by, for now?"** It already had the letter **e** typed in it, because the E I pressed to talk leaked into the text box. I deleted it, typed Wren, clicked "that'll do". WRITTEN ON THE COVER: WREN. Nell then said "What do I call you? — You don't know. Course you don't." after I had named myself, so it read out of order.

More E on Nell: "Well. Go on."; the corner label "NELL — RUN" with "hold shift to run"; NEW JOB: GET IT HOME, "NELL — GET ON THE HORSE", "E — GET ON THE HORSE WHEN IT COMES".

A bull walked up and stood on top of me ("Oof. It knows me."). A horse trotted in. I pressed E and was on it.

Two buttons appeared: **1 · GO ON, THEN.** and **2 · I'LL HANDLE IT.** I did not really know what either meant. I picked 2 because I wanted to do the thing myself. "NELL SITS DOWN ON HER BASKET. THE GATE IS YOURS." and "LEAD HIM WELL IN. THEN E AT THE GATE SHUTS IT."

I galloped off, overshot and lost the bull. The map is pretty, but the labels near me were printed on top of each other and unreadable. I got "THE GAP IS UNDER ITS NAME: THE FIELD GATE." I dragged the camera round, found the bull, and led him through the gap. At gameSec 60 I was in a field with a bull behind my horse.

**At gameSec 60, can I say what this game wants me to do?**
- **Right now: yes, 9/10 sure.** Lead the bull through the field gate and shut it.
- **In the large: 4/10 sure.** I woke on a bench after three years away and don't know my name. Twelve lands on one sheet, and presumably I walk them and find out who I was. Nothing has told me this.

## 2. What I understood, and how

- **Who I am.** *Told* by the note and Nell's first four lines. The best onboarding-by-fiction I've seen in a while.
- **The world.** *Told* by the signpost at gameSec 210 and the map footer.
- **The goal.** Short-term Nell's job. Long-term *guessed*.
- **Walk / look / act.** *Told* in one line at the right moment, after I had already stood up.
- **Run.** *Told* when Nell says run. **Standing up.** *Guessed.*
- **Horse.** E to mount *told*; WHOA then GET OFF *read from the prompt*; shift to gallop *guessed*. Very fast; I lost my bearings the first time.
- **M, N, wheel, Esc.** All *guessed*.
- **E with nothing prompted** gives "Closer, and it says GET ON THE HORSE." A nice nudge.
- **The "✓ VERB" toast** only means "you pressed E on that prompt". I got "✓ SHUT THE GATE" ten times while a bubble said "Not yet. He's not in."
- **Hedges.** Never understood. Big see-through blobs with a wavy line. I couldn't tell where they start, whether they're solid, or where the gap is. "In" never felt like a place.
- **What "in" means for the bull.** Never understood, nor how to shut a gate on an animal that is glued to me.

## 4. What reacted, and what didn't

**Answered:** Nell on standing, on the note, on my choice ("Course you will.", "Your gate. You said."), on my nagging; the name on the cover; Nell actually sits on her basket; the bull bumps and follows; a goat tags along; prints stay on the ground; "Solid."; FOUND toasts; the crossroads' title; the signpost's card and toast; "Closer, and it says …"; carts on the road.

**Nothing visible:** the first two E presses on Nell (over a second each); E at the gate ten times (a ✓ toast that reads as success, the leaf never moved); the carter and the handcart man; the well (✓ and nothing on screen); map labels stacked into one smudge; floating names far from their owners (NELL a third of a screen from Nell; "SHUTHEIGATGATE").

## 5. What I wanted to do and couldn't

- **Shut the gate.** The bull sticks to me whether I walk, sprint or gallop. After "I'll handle it" there is no way to say "actually Nell, you do it".
- **See the field.** The translucent hedges made it worse.
- **Talk to the carter.**
- **Know what 1 and 2 meant before choosing.** Go on with what? Handle what?
- **Get the bull off me.** He stands exactly on top of my character.
- **Hear or see the well answer.**

## 6. Best and worst moment

**Best:** the note, followed straight away by Nell's "You said you'd only be gone an hour." Runner-up: the signpost.

**Worst:** gameSec 44 to 189 at the field gate. Ten attempts, the same answer, a tick-mark toast that looked like success, a bull on my head, a field with no visible edges. A real player quits here, in the second minute.

## 7. To a friend

"It's a pencil-drawn little open world where you wake up on a park bench three years after you said you'd be back in an hour, and everyone's a bit short with you about it. The writing is dry and lovely and the world notices what you do, but the first job, getting a bull through a gate, had me going in circles for more than two minutes and I never did shut the thing."

Three things next time: 1. Start over and press **1** to see Nell shut the gate. 2. Ride north through the gate of Brim. 3. Follow the signpost arm that says 8:15. (A fourth: unstick the first page of the notebook.)
