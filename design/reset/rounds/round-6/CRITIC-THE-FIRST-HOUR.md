For forty seconds INKLANDS teaches the way A Short Hike does: STAND UP, Nell's "RUN.", "hold shift to run" arriving exactly as the bull does, a horse, a gate, "There. That's more like you." Verb, reason, payoff. Then the strip falls apart where Hike never does. The horse I was just given sits rooted at the long fence (frames 033 and 034 are identical, GET OFF the only word) and again at the belfry, with no message; later it stands in somebody's dining room beside a dresser (090-091). The second job is literally "WAIT HERE FOR THE LAMPS". "NIGHT. THE MONSTERS ARE OUT. GET INDOORS." fires with no monster and no door that answers. The stone's purpose is posted at the tarn after it was spent on a pigeon that never flinched; "3 OF 12 KEPT" rewards merely arriving. The player lost a third of his session hunting a belfry on a map whose labels float off their pins while the camera turned under him. Hike keeps Hawk Peak in view every second, and its first golden feather is a capability felt within two minutes; INKLANDS' new capability after five minutes is a wait button. The morning market (096-097: bread on the trestles, "It was never money") beats anything in Hike's opening, and I would still rather be climbing.
